Talk to our team
What should I expect during my first consultation?

A relaxed, unhurried consultation — no drills, no pressure. Dr. Ashley reviews your dental and medical history, performs a full oral health screening and discusses your goals, using digital X-rays and 3D imaging. You leave with a personalized plan and full cost transparency.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

Yes — for new and existing patients, subject to availability. We also offer same-day CEREC crowns: designed, milled and placed in a single visit. Call in the morning and we'll do our best to fit you in that day.

Is the pain-free guarantee real?

Absolutely. We combine topical anesthetics, ultra-fine needles and a slow-delivery injection technique, and offer nitrous oxide for anxious patients. If you feel pain at any point, we stop immediately and adjust — no exceptions.

How much does a smile makeover cost?

It depends on the treatments involved — whitening starts around $350, while a full veneer set typically ranges from $8,000 to $18,000. You get a detailed, itemized quote at your consultation, plus flexible financing and most major insurance plans for applicable procedures.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

With proper care, 12 to 20 years. We use only laboratory-crafted feldspathic or e.max porcelain, provide a custom night guard if needed and schedule regular check-ins to keep results perfect long-term.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, most PPO plans — and we handle the claims for you. Cosmetic procedures are usually not covered, but preventive care, exams, X-rays and some restorative treatments often are. We verify your coverage before your appointment.

Where are you located, and what are your hours?

In Brickell, Miami — 253 NE 2nd St, Suite 1710. Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 and Saturday 9:00–14:00, with parking in the building's garage, a short walk from Brickell City Centre.
