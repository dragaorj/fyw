What is an LLC?

A Limited Liability Company is the most popular structure for foreign founders. It separates your personal assets from the business, has flexible taxation and can be owned 100% by non‑residents.

What is a C Corp?

A C Corporation is taxed at the corporate level and can issue shares. It is the standard for startups that plan to raise money from investors.

Which state should I choose?

It depends on your goals. Delaware is the gold standard for startups raising funds; Wyoming and Florida are popular for low fees and simple maintenance. We help you decide.

Does Globalfy work with all types of companies?

We work with entrepreneurs from any country opening LLCs or C Corps in the US, from solo freelancers to companies with several owners.
