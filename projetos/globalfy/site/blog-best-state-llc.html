What is the best state to form an LLC as a foreign founder?

Globalfy teamUpdated Sep 2026 · 6 min read
Key takeaways
  • There is no single best state: it depends on where you operate and whether you will raise money.
  • Delaware is the reference for investors; Wyoming and New Mexico are known for simple maintenance.
  • If you will have an office or employees in a state, you may need to register there too.

The popular states at a glance

StateKnown forGood fit if…
DelawareBusiness courts and investor familiarityYou plan to raise venture capital
WyomingLow costs and privacyYou run an online business from abroad
New MexicoVery light yearly maintenanceYou want the simplest upkeep
FloridaProximity to Latin AmericaYou will operate or sell in Florida
TexasNo state personal income tax, big marketYou will have presence in Texas

Where you operate matters

Forming in one state and doing business physically in another usually means registering as a foreign entity in the second one (foreign qualification) — and paying fees in both. If you will have an office, warehouse or employees somewhere, consider forming there.

Look at the yearly costs, not only the filing fee

Formation fees are paid once; annual reports, franchise taxes and registered agent fees are paid every year. Compare the total cost over three years before deciding.

How to decide in three questions

  • Will you raise money from investors? → Delaware C Corp.
  • Will you operate physically in a state? → Form there.
  • Online business run from abroad? → Wyoming or New Mexico LLC.
