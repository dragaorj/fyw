New requirement

Beneficial Ownership Information: new filing requirement for US businesses

The BOI report is a requirement for companies in the US enforced by FinCEN. It provides information about a company's owners to prevent illegal activities such as money laundering and fraud. Most private businesses are required to file this report — that's why we included it in all our plans.
